What this data can and cannot tell you

These numbers are self-reported checkboxes. A student can tick four boxes in ten seconds without doing any work, and nothing here would look different from genuine study. Treat this dashboard as an engagement signal — who has gone quiet, who is stuck on a phase, who needs a nudge — not as evidence of learning.

The programme's real evidence is the day-NN-topic folders each student commits to GitHub, with code, tests and a README. Verify against those repositories before grading. A student whose dashboard shows 40 days but whose repository has 6 commits is telling you something useful.

Also note that Syncs counts how many times progress was written. A row with 45 days complete but only 2 syncs means the work was marked in one or two sittings rather than daily.
